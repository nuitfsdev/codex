## Candidate: BTVA — Budgeted Topology Violation Audit (secondary, not main)
**Hypothesis:** testing whether chosen seed/unnamed-answer/support bundles preserve all question-required spatial relation endpoints and reject competing same-class topology could diagnose selection failures and improve accuracy only if used as a low-cost reranker.
**Stage:** pre-render selector verification; no change to PoseRecover/renderer.
**Object-selection rule:** parse question into typed relation constraints; retrieve named seeds semantically, search unnamed answer candidates across ALL fixed boxes with soft relation compatibility; rank candidate witness bundles by normalized relation satisfaction and topology consistency; select at most K physical objects. Never use oracle answer IDs or render full scene.
**Supporting-context rule:** preserve relation endpoints, intermediate anchors, competing same-class objects needed to disambiguate, and optional context reserve. Do not equate spatial proximity with support.
**Expected benefit:** higher answer-witness recall/complete-witness coverage at matched K, NOT yet measured.
**Difficulty:** medium. **Training:** none. **Compute:** CPU geometry O(N^2) pair scores; optional small beam O(BK^2); selector GPU VRAM ~0; whole VLM peak VRAM unknown, T4 16GB target for 3B/4B quantized; no guarantee for 7B.
**Data/metrics:** ScanQA validation then SQA3D; EM/CIDEr (ScanQA), accuracy (SQA3D), ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC, answer-witness recall, SCR only with independent GT, complete-witness coverage, context-loss rate, calls/latency/peak VRAM. For counting/negation, full metadata completeness check must be flagged as a separate setting.
**Ablation:** Semantic Top-K vs OpenSlotSoft vs Role-Aware vs BTVA at K=5/10/20, M=1, fixed selected-instance-mesh renderer, VLM/prompt/calls; topology-off, context-reserve-off, same-class-disambiguation-off; independent witness annotations 50–100 questions.
**Risks:** boxes misrepresent support/occlusion; wrong reference frame; parser errors; same-class ambiguity; scoring may overfit; multiple valid witness sets; high context-loss at K=5.
**Publication potential:** low standalone because CitySTAR already has topology verification; moderate only if novel QA-specific pre-render witness utility beats strong matched-budget grounding-pruning baselines.
