## Candidate: Paired Witness-Evidence Density Audit (PWEDA), evaluation only
Hypothesis: matched-K scenes with high vs low *independent* complete-witness coverage yield QA differences attributable to evidence completeness rather than object count. Stage: selector evaluation, renderer. Selection rule: use unchanged Semantic Top-K/OpenSlotSoft/Role-Aware selector, never oracle object IDs during selection; keep |S|<=K. Supporting context: independently annotate named seeds, unnamed answer witnesses, alternative supporting bundles; avoid exact noun matching. Expected benefit: causal diagnostic, not assumed QA gain. Difficulty medium; CPU annotation/geometry, 0 incremental selector GPU VRAM; VLM sequential T4 16GB 3B-4B 4-bit target, actual peak unknown. ScanQA val first 50-100 examples, then SQA3D; metrics EM/CIDEr, ORR=1-Kselected/N, QEC, AWR, independently annotated SCR/CWC, paired QA delta, latency and VRAM. Ablate paired high-vs-low witness coverage at matched K=10 M=1, same camera/renderer/model/prompt, remove-support vs remove-distractor; K=5/20 later. Risks: selecting matched pairs from ground truth may bias analysis, counting/negation require global completeness, non-unique witness sets, occlusion confounding. Publication potential low standalone; moderate as robust evidence for main paper. Scores /5 (compute higher=cheaper): novelty 1.2, expected gain 1.0, feasibility 4.3, compute 4.4, fit 4.9, evidence 3.8. Context loss risk high K=5, unknown at K=10.
