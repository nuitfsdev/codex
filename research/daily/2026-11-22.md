## Sources audited
- **Thinking with Visual Grounding** — Findings EMNLP 2026 (October), https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1418/ . Grounding-aware reinforcement learning ties intermediate visual references to evidence, including counting/spatial reasoning; Gemma3-4B-IT studied. This is a trained 2D grounding method, **not** a training-free 3D pre-render selector, and does not transfer as a method without training. Transferable insight: audit whether the 2D VLM actually uses selected witness evidence, rather than labels or language priors. Official code, VRAM and ScanQA transfer unverified.

## Secondary candidate: Witness-Evidence Dependence Audit (WEDA)
**Research hypothesis:** Apparent gains from object selection may be driven by rendered class labels, semantic priors or camera artifacts, not retention of the objects that actually support the answer. Causal evidence removal can diagnose this.
**Stage:** Evaluation and spatial verification, not main object selection.
**Object selection rule:** Freeze SAFER-QA selection S (strict K, only selected objects rendered); named seeds identified by query parsing, unnamed answer candidates found scene-wide from fixed boxes, not exact noun match.
**Supporting-context rule:** Independent annotators identify alternative admissible witness bundles and competing anchors; for a diagnostic ablation remove one witness while holding total K constant by substituting a matched distractor. Do not confuse a missing answer candidate with a missing named seed.
**Expected benefit:** Stronger evidence for causal necessity of supporting context and honest failure characterization; no direct QA accuracy gain expected.
**Difficulty:** Low-medium for CPU metadata, medium for manual annotation and additional VLM calls. No selector training, no GPU benchmark daily. End-to-end target 4B quantized on T4 16GB; incremental VRAM roughly unchanged at equal image count, latency/inference cost scales with ablation variants; actual peak VRAM unknown.
**Data/metrics:** 50–100 independently annotated ScanQA relational questions, optional SQA3D. EM, CIDEr, ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC, SCR (only with independent GT), answer-witness recall, complete-witness coverage, context-loss rate, counterfactual answer-change rate, latency/VRAM.
**Ablation:** fixed K=10 M=1 and same pose/prompt/renderer/backbone: semantic Top-K, CAPruner-adapted, SAFER-QA; then SAFER-QA original vs remove annotated supporting witness vs remove equally ranked non-witness vs label-off, keeping K matched. Avoid using ScanQA relevance annotation as exhaustive support GT.
**Risks:** multiple valid witness sets, answer paraphrase variability, accidental visibility differences, labels/geometry shortcuts. Publication potential low standalone; valuable validation for main paper.
**Scores (subjective 1–5; compute higher=cheaper):** Novelty 1.2, Expected Accuracy Gain 1.0, Feasibility 4.3, Compute Cost 4.0, Fit 4.8, Evidence Strength 4.0. ORR/QEC/SCR are measured quantities, not established results; context loss risk medium/high at K=5.
