## Decision
**KEEP SAFER-QA / QASER-PreRender as the main hypothesis, but LOWER the novelty claim for generic question-conditioned object pruning.** A directly relevant **peer-reviewed** work, **3D-SceneQ** (IEEE Transactions on Multimedia 2026; arXiv first posted **27 Oct 2026**, surfaced in the **28 Oct cs.CV new listing**), explicitly learns question-guided object pruning and evaluates on **ScanQA/SQA3D**. It is more relevant than most prior mining candidates and MUST be included in the related-work/novelty audit. No GPU benchmark today. No claim of improved QA for our method yet.

**Important distinction:** 3D-SceneQ prunes **already extracted 3D object tokens** inside a trained 3D LLM; SAFER-QA proposes **before-rendering physical object/subscene selection** for a frozen <=7B **2D VLM**, retaining **unnamed QA answer/support witnesses** with fixed annotated object boxes, and measuring downstream QA under equal K,M,VLM-call budgets. That distinction is testable, not automatic novelty. QGAP's published retention formula keeps 50–100% of tokens, unlike a strict small K when N is large. Avoid claiming 'question-conditioned object selection', 'adaptive pruning', '2D/3D fusion' or 'QA on SQA3D' as novel in isolation.

### Paper 1 — 3D-SceneQ (NEWLY IDENTIFIED DIRECT PRIOR ART)
**Yongqi Shan, Lu Zhang, Jiazuo Yu, Yunzhi Zhuge, Huchuan Lu.** *3D-SceneQ: Empowering 3D LLM With Query-Guided Adaptive Pruning and Multi-Modal Feature Enhancement*. **IEEE Transactions on Multimedia**, vol.28, pp.5972–5983 (2026), DOI **10.1109/TMM.2026.3668502**; arXiv submitted **27 Oct 2026**, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37202 , full HTML https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37202v1 . Journal status is independently corroborated by arXiv journal-ref/DOI and dblp https://dblp.org/rec/journals/tmm/ShanZYZL26 . **No official runnable code/release found or linked in manuscript; public code NOT verified.** Data are standard Scan2Cap, ScanQA, SQA3D plus alignment/instruction datasets; no new downloadable benchmark identified. 2-stage 3D-language alignment and instruction tuning, NOT training-free. **Vicuna-7B**, PointNet++, OpenSeg 2D features, BERT embeddings; **QGAP 7.1M**, **MOFE 70.3M**, **122.6M trainable parameters** total, LoRA r=16, **2x NVIDIA A800 GPUs**, 10 epochs. **Peak VRAM not given**; original training unsuitable for T4.

**Exact QGAP:** q_emb=BERT(question), object embeddings=BERT(predicted object labels), rank by cosine. Learned latent query LQ + cross/self attention produces gamma=sigmoid(...); k=round((gamma/2+0.5)*n). Hence k/n in (0.5,1) up to rounding, **maximum nominal object-token reduction ~50%**; not a strict K<=10 pre-render object policy. Note predicted labels in original; thesis fixed GT labels/boxes would be a different setting. MOFE projects OpenSeg multi-view pixel features onto 3D and pools per object before LLM fusion; segmentation/perception not our contribution.

**Reported results (NOT directly comparable with our model):** full 3D-SceneQ ScanQA val **CIDEr 104.7**, SQA3D test **EM@1 67.0%**; LEO-like baseline **101.4 CIDEr / 50.0% EM**. QGAP-only ablation **101.6 / 70.1%**; MOFE-only **102.0 / 66.7%**; random filter **90.1 / 68.9%**. Important: **QGAP-only SQA3D 70.1 > full model 67.0**; cannot attribute all gains to fusion, and task trade-offs are non-monotonic. Table III ScanQA BLEU-4=13.6 vs Table IV full-model=15.2: minor internal discrepancy; prefer CIDEr/EM. No ORR/QEC/SCR or supporting-object ground truth is established by the paper. Source sections III-B, IV-A, Tables III–V.

**Novelty boundary:** QA-conditioned pruning of 3D object tokens and adaptive k already peer-reviewed on exact thesis benchmarks. New defensible claim must be *explicit unnamed QA witness retention before any partial-scene rendering* under strict small object budget with frozen 2D VLM and matched-budget metrics. Compare QGAP-inspired frozen BERT question-to-class Top-K baseline (NOT trained QGAP reproduction) and ideally cite published 3D-SceneQ as contextual 3D LLM baseline with non-equivalent input/training.

## Object selection research track — direct implications
- **Named seeds**: explicit question entities; frozen BGE/MiniLM or BERT label similarity, category synonyms, instance ambiguity reserve.
- **Unnamed answer slots**: search ALL fixed annotated objects using relation/role compatibility, not only noun matches; use question reference frame and competing hypotheses.
- **Unnamed supporting/context**: retain relation endpoints, bridges, disambiguators, frame anchors, alternative plausible answers under |S|<=K; render ONLY S.
- QGAP directly covers semantic question-to-object relevance and adaptive token pruning; DOES NOT demonstrate QA-specific independent support-witness recall or pre-render K<<N, but absence of evidence is NOT proof of novelty.
- Explicit baseline **QGAP-like Frozen Label Top-K**: BERT(question) vs BERT(GT class label) cosine, fixed K, NO trained latent-query ratio and NO support expansion. Equivalent to a stricter semantic Top-K baseline; do NOT mislabel as official QGAP reproduction.
- Control adaptive budgets at **equal mean object budget** and **equal total image/VLM calls**; 3D-SceneQ original 50%-100% retention is not equal to K=10 for many ScanNet scenes.
- Continue ORR=1-|S|/N; QEC for named GT IDs; AWR for unnamed answer IDs; SCR only with independent support labels (otherwise PROXY); Complete-Witness Coverage; Context-Loss Rate; QA vs K; latency/peak VRAM; scene-bootstrap confidence intervals. K={5,10,20}, M={1,2}. High context loss K=5; medium/high K=10 for multi-hop/counting. ORR 50–85% and QEC>=95% are UNVERIFIED TARGETS.

## Candidate A — QGAP-Aware Matched-Budget Baseline (BASELINE, not main novelty)
**Hypothesis:** A frozen question-to-category semantic Top-K, reflecting QGAP's ranking component without training, may already provide much of the gain from removing distractors; Role-Aware Witness Coverage must outperform it at identical K and M to justify complexity.
**Changed stage:** Pre-render object ranking baseline. **Object rule:** BERT(question)-BERT(GT class labels) cosine Top-K; no extra detector/segmenter; optionally seed-protected variant as separate ablation. **Supporting rule:** no explicit support expansion in pure baseline; score unnamed AWR/SCR to expose failures. **Benefit:** rigorous falsifiable stronger prior-art comparison, NOT assumed accuracy improvement. **Difficulty:** low. **Training:** none. **Compute/VRAM:** CPU embedding cache + cosine; ~0 incremental GPU VRAM; VLM cost unchanged; T4 feasibility unmeasured. **Data:** ScanQA/SQA3D val. **Ablations:** semantic Top-K (MiniLM vs BERT), seed protection, RWC support reserve; matched K=5/10/20, M=1/2; ORR/QEC/AWR/SCR/context loss, CIDEr/EM, time, VRAM. **Risk:** class-only embeddings miss attributes, relation and unnamed answer; context loss HIGH for relational/counting. **Publication:** NONE standalone, essential baseline. **Scores /5 (subjective):** Novelty 1.0, Expected Gain 2.7, Feasibility 4.9, Compute 4.9 (higher cheaper), Fit 4.9, Evidence 4.8.

## Candidate B — Pose-Counterfactual Witness Audit (EVALUATION REFINEMENT)
**Hypothesis:** PoseAlign-T gains on situated QA should correlate with correct heading-conditioned answer changes; random-pose invariance may expose language/label shortcuts even when EM improves.
**Changed stages:** PoseAlign-T, view selection, spatial verification and evaluation; NO change to main pre-render selector. **Object rule:** fixed S from SAFER-QA; same named seeds and unnamed witness IDs across valid heading interventions, render only S. **Supporting rule:** preserve same reference-frame anchors, competing objects and relation endpoints; do not introduce full-scene images. **Expected benefit:** evidence of genuine pose use, catches frame mismatch; QA gain uncertain. **Difficulty:** low-medium. **Training:** none. **Compute:** CPU geometry rotations/verification; VLM re-inference for a small sampled test set, peak VRAM unchanged if sequential, latency/calls increase; no verified T4 number. **Data:** SQA3D validation subset, optionally Real-3DQA counterfactual headings (if accessible). **Metrics:** paired EM, pose-dependence delta, counterfactual consistency, frame-direction accuracy, ORR/QEC/AWR/SCR inherited, context loss, latency/VRAM. **Ablations:** correct pose vs shuffled pose vs yaw +90/+180; pose-shuffle with and without situation text; box-only/label-only/render-only; match object K and view M; use valid counterfactual labels for changed heading (not original GT blindly). **Risks:** questions that are pose-invariant, ambiguous language, pose mismatch; changing pose can change visibility; do not equate large drop under invalid pose with success. **Publication:** LOW standalone, MEDIUM as rigorous evaluation within main thesis. **Scores:** Novelty 1.7, Gain 2.6, Feasibility 4.6, Compute 4.5, Fit 4.8, Evidence 4.7.
