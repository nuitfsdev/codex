## Paper 1: PROVE — what transfers and what does not
Method: frozen CLIP ViT-B/16 (~150M parameters) produces patch features, text embedding, Grad-ECLIP prior; a **TRAINED** lightweight evidential head predicts Beta(alpha,beta) per patch. Mean alpha/(alpha+beta) is relevance; vacuity 2/(alpha+beta) quantifies low evidence; absence score -max_patch(alpha+beta) detects no referent. Training uses fractional patch-mask coverage, evidence regularization, and no-target prior-pull; gRefCOCO training, transfer tests on Flickr30k Entities/PhraseCut. Not training-free as a whole, despite frozen backbone. Code includes data prep, training, Q1/Q2/Q3 evaluation scripts and ablations. GPU/VRAM numbers are not stated; original compute used HPC resources, so **no verified single-GPU requirement**. For a potential small local adaptation, budget 4–8 GB for CLIP+head standalone as a *planning estimate only*, profile before commitment. No ScanQA/SQA3D or 3D grounding results. Paper's gRefCOCO testB absence AUROC about 0.813 (ViT-B/16), **not a 3D QA score**. Core novelty boundary: calibrated absence/abstention with probabilistic patch evidence already has strong prior art. Crucial caution: “object absent from selected render” != “object absent from 3D scene”; a poor selector or camera can cause false absence. Thus use PROVE only for optional **post-render evidence sufficiency / visibility diagnostic**, not a pre-render main selector.

## Paper 2: MV-ScanQA / TripAlign — direct benchmark and novelty audit
MV-ScanQA: approximately **10K** composed QA pairs; **68%** require >=2 original egocentric views, **13%** >=3, versus about **6%** of ScanQA questions requiring >1 view in the authors' solvability analysis. This is a **dataset-specific, visibility-based estimate** (IoSA >0.5 of projected object box, not a guarantee of true occlusion-free visibility). Source questions share a contextual anchor and have complementary relevant-object sets. Example: “What is on the right of the small desk where the wooden chair is in front of?” needs multi-hop bridge/witness objects. The project UI shows “All Related Objects”, “Minimum Views Required” and oracle views; these are **NOT independently certified exhaustive support GT**. TripAlign: 1M <2D view, set of 3D objects, text> triplets; view-grounded multi-object correspondence, **prior art** for object-set/context alignment. Official code DATA.md provides precomputation of scene-view-object IoSA ratios and question-based view ranking; note repo TODO still lists some missing view-selection code/docs/assets. The original LEGO uses 3D encoder/detector features + 2D Fuyu and LoRA training; official README says typical training **4–8 GPUs of ~40GB VRAM and 150–300GB RAM**, not thesis feasible. It reports cleaned-script reproduced MV-ScanQA EM **33.7**, ScanQA val EM **28.3**; these are **LEGO** results and cannot be compared directly with our unrun pipeline. Official repo has dataset and checkpoints links but download integrity/access not yet checked. Transfer **benchmark/data protocol only**, not LEGO architecture or pretraining. Do NOT treat TripAlign object sets as complete supporting-context labels without auditing.

## Candidate A — View-Complete Witness Allocation (VCWA), refinement of Role-Aware Witness Coverage
**Research Hypothesis:** Under fixed object budget K and view budget M, jointly retaining complete named-seed + unnamed-answer/support *witness bundles* and allocating their visibility across M selected views yields better multi-hop QA than independently ranked objects and per-object view selection.
**Changed stage:** pre-render question-conditioned object selector + PoseAlign-T-conditioned viewpoint allocation; selected-instance-mesh Abstract3D remains unchanged.
**Object-selection rule:** parse q into explicit named seeds, open answer slots, relation chain, attributes, frame; use frozen MiniLM/BGE-small + synonyms for seeds; search **all annotated object metadata** for unnamed candidate endpoints, bridge objects, competing candidates; build relation-hypothesis witness sets W_h and choose their union S under |S|<=K. No exact-noun or KNN hard gate.
**Supporting-context rule:** retain bridge objects, relation endpoints, seed disambiguators and necessary frame anchors even if not explicitly named; allow **different** views to witness different members of the same bundle. Do not require every related object to co-appear in a single image. Avoid including irrelevant structural anchors. Uncertain pose -> keep alternative witnesses, never claim completeness from box projection alone.
**Proposed objective (NOT from the paper):** maximize sum_h u_h * 1[W_h subset S] * min_{o in W_h} max_{v in V} vis(o,v) - lambda*redundancy(S,V), subject to |S|<=K, |V|<=M. vis may initially use frustum/box IoSA; refine with low-res selected-mesh depth/occlusion checks. For relational edges, add co-visibility or explicit cross-view identity consistency if needed. Crucially, candidate camera scoring must not render the full scene; only object metadata and selected meshes may be used.
**Expected benefit:** higher complete-witness visibility and multi-hop QA, especially where M=1 misses necessary evidence; UNMEASURED.
**Training/compute:** V0 inference-only, cached semantic embeddings and CPU geometry, approximate O(N² + N*M_candidate) preprocessing; no selector GPU needed. Additional low-res selected-mesh visibility rasterization possible. 2B/4B quantized VLM ~8–16GB as **planning target**, M=2/4 increases image tokens/latency, profile. Difficulty **medium-high**.
**Data/metrics:** ScanQA val and SQA3D val primary; MV-ScanQA held-out split as targeted multi-view stress test (subject to verifying schema and splits). QA CIDEr/EM (ScanQA), SQA3D EM, MV-ScanQA EM, ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC for named seeds, Answer-Witness Recall, Supporting-Context Retention **only true GT with independent support labels** (else PROXY), Complete-Witness-Visibility@M, bridge retention, context-loss, latency, peak VRAM, view redundancy. Dataset “related object” sets may serve as silver proxy only after auditing.
**Ablation:** matched K={5,10,20}; M={1,2} ScanQA/SQA3D and {1,2,4} MV-ScanQA; Semantic Top-K, OpenSlotSoft, Role-Aware Witness Coverage, per-object best view, fixed PoseAlign-T, VCWA with/without bridge retention, with/without visibility score, with/without occlusion-aware refinement. Keep same VLM/prompt/renderer/image resolution and number of VLM calls **within each M**. M=4 vs M=1 is a budget curve, not matched compute. Paired scene-bootstrap CIs, label+box-only controls, support-removal and random replacement. Original egocentric view solvability is not an upper bound for arbitrary novel camera renders.
**Risk:** projected-box IoSA overstates visibility; wrong object ID across views; K too small for multi-hop; rendering a partial scene changes occlusion; dataset synthesis noise; M=4 cost. Context-loss HIGH K=5, MEDIUM-HIGH K=10. **Publication:** LOW standalone (view/object-set alignment prior art), MEDIUM as integrated SAFER-QA refinement if QA-vs-K,M gains are significant and robust.
**Scores /5:** Novelty **2.6**, Expected Accuracy Gain **3.4**, Feasibility **4.2**, Compute Cost **4.3** (higher=cheaper), Fit **4.9**, Evidence Strength **4.4**. Scores not empirical.

## Candidate B — Presence-versus-Visibility Evidence Gate (PVEG), secondary diagnostic
**Hypothesis:** distinguishing “referent not in annotated scene” from “referent omitted by selector / outside selected camera view” reduces unjustified negative answers and identifies context loss.
**Stage:** post-render evidence verification/abstention, not main pre-render selector.
**Object rule:** unchanged SAFER-QA selected S. Metadata-only candidate presence checks are run on all fixed annotations but do not render omitted objects. **Supporting rule:** preserve named seeds and unnamed witnesses, never infer global absence from empty selected render. If low visual evidence and scene-wide candidate exists, mark insufficient / consider a separately budgeted refresh; otherwise report null under well-defined metadata-completeness assumptions.
**Expected benefit:** better negative/existence QA calibration and debugging, not proven.
**Implementation:** V0 training-free symbolic presence-vs-view check (CPU); optional *PROVE adapter* requires light supervised training and CLIP+Grad-ECLIP features (not inference-only original method). Original VRAM not reported; tentative standalone 4–8GB for CLIP+head profiling, plus sequential VLM ~8–16GB target; difficulty low-medium for V0, medium-high for trained PROVE integration.
**Metrics/ablation:** ScanQA/SQA3D negative/existence probes, absence AUROC, selective accuracy/risk-coverage, false-negative rate, QA EM, ORR/QEC/SCR, context-loss, latency/VRAM; no gate vs symbolic gate vs image-only confidence vs optional PROVE, with/without support removal, selected vs omitted objects. Risk: incomplete annotations, domain shift from natural images to abstract meshes, false visual absence, supervision leakage. Publication LOW standalone.
**Scores /5:** Novelty **1.9**, Gain **2.7**, Feasibility **3.4**, Compute **3.5**, Fit **4.4**, Evidence **4.2**.
