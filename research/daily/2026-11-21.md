## Candidate: Ambiguity-Preserving Witness Reserve (APWR) — secondary refinement
**Hypothesis:** At matched physical-object budget, preserving same-class competitors and unnamed relation witnesses before render improves QA on ambiguous relational questions versus hard top-1 seed grounding.
**Stage:** question parser -> pre-render selection; optional pose-conditioned spatial compatibility.
**Selection rule:** parse named seed *categories* and relation slots, keep plausible seed instance hypotheses; retrieve unnamed answer candidates scene-wide via semantic retrieval and soft 3D box relation tests; never exact noun-only or KNN hard gate. Rank witness bundles and select their union up to K=10, reserving 1–3 slots for same-class competing anchors; do not render excluded objects.
**Supporting-context rule:** preserve relation endpoints, alternative anchors, and candidates satisfying open answer slots; for counting/negation require global metadata completeness or abstain from completeness claims.
**Expected benefit:** fewer false confident omissions of alternate seed instances; not measured.
**Difficulty:** medium; training-free; CPU pairwise geometry O(N^2) worst case; embeddings can be cached; no dedicated GPU for selector. End-to-end VLM peak VRAM unmeasured, target 4B quantized on T4 16GB; extra VLM calls none in V0.
**Evaluation:** ScanQA relational ambiguous subset and SQA3D; independent 50–100 question witness annotations. Fixed K=5,10,20 and M=1. Compare semantic Top-K, CAPruner-adapted, SAFER-QA without reserve, + reserve 1/2/3; fixed pose/renderer/backbone/prompt. Report EM, CIDEr, ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC, independently annotated SCR, answer-witness recall, complete-witness coverage, latency and peak VRAM; report context-loss rate by question type.
**Risks:** wasted budget on distractors, context loss at K=5, wrong viewpoint frame, annotation incompleteness; reserve can hurt simple questions. Publication potential low standalone, medium only if matched-budget gains robustly exceed strong baselines.
**Scores (subjective 1-5; compute higher=cheaper):** Novelty 1.5; Expected Accuracy Gain 3.0; Feasibility 4.6; Compute Cost 4.8; Fit 5.0; Evidence Strength 3.8. ORR target 50–85% unverified; QEC >=95% unverified; SCR unknown; context loss medium-high under tight K.
