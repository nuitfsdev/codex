## Relevant prior-work audit
- **EgoAlign** (arXiv:2610.16176, submitted 12 Oct 2026): agent-centric coordinate alignment; reports pose shuffling scarcely affects some earlier models, while its trained alignment increases pose sensitivity. Not training-free; useful as a *pose-dependence diagnostic*, not a model to port. Code, exact VRAM, peer-reviewed venue not verified today.
- **VIZOR** (arXiv:2602.00637, submitted 31 Jan 2026): training-free viewpoint-invariant scene graphs with object-centric front directions, evaluated on Replica/Nr3D; prior art for generic relation graph construction. Code/VRAM/venue not verified today.
- **ViewMind3D** (arXiv:2607.28442, submitted 30 Jul 2026): training-free question-driven multi-view selection and structured QA on ScanQA/SQA3D; known prior, not a new finding. Reported ScanQA CIDEr 73.4 in authors' setup; no direct matched comparison.
Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16176 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00637 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28442

## Candidate — Pose-Dependence and Witness-Visibility Audit (PDWVA), evaluation only
**Hypothesis:** some apparent gains from pose alignment / object selection may instead come from labels or geometry shortcuts; perturbing pose and holding selected physical objects fixed will expose whether reasoning is pose-sensitive.
**Changed stages:** PoseRecover/PoseAlign-T, view selection and evaluation; no detector/segmenter change.
**Selection rule:** freeze the same selected S from semantic Top-K / OpenSlotSoft / Role-Aware selector for each counterfactual. Explicit seeds from query semantics; unnamed answer candidates discovered scene-wide by soft geometry, not exact noun or hard KNN.
**Supporting rule:** preserve independent named seeds, unnamed answer objects, relation endpoints, competing instances and alternative witness bundles. Only render S with |S|<=K, never whole scene for main method.
**Expected benefit:** diagnostic causal attribution, not direct accuracy gain.
**Difficulty:** medium. **Training:** none. **Compute:** CPU rigid transforms / box projection and extra render/VLM passes; selector extra VRAM negligible, whole 3B–4B quantized VLM peak not measured. On T4 16GB feasibility must be checked in experiment, not assumed.
**Evaluation:** ScanQA/SQA3D spatial-direction subset; EM/CIDEr, pose-sensitivity accuracy delta, witness visibility, ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC, answer-witness recall, SCR (GT only with independent annotation), complete-witness coverage, context loss, latency, GPU peak VRAM.
**Ablations:** true pose vs shuffled pose vs rotated camera while holding S and K=10, M=1; labels on/off; PoseAlign-T on/off; then M=2 and K=5/20. Respect coordinate-frame transformations and do not compare invalid counterfactuals.
**Risk:** perturbations can alter visibility and occlusion; pose-dependent questions require agent frame; missing support annotations; counting/negation need global-completeness check. Context loss high at K=5, unmeasured.
**Publication potential:** low alone, moderate as controlled evaluation supporting main method.
