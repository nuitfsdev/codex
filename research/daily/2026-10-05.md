# Daily Proposal Research — 2026-10-05

## High-value update: topology-aware support retention

### Primary evidence
**Uncovering and Shaping the Latent Representation of 3D Scene Topology in Vision-Language Models** — Haoming Wang, Wei Gao. NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight; arXiv:2605.07148; official code: https://github.com/pittisl/vlm-latent-shaping.

Key result: VLM latent spatial representations correspond to topology-preserving graph structure; a Dirichlet-energy regularizer over a 3D Gaussian-kernel graph improves real-world spatial benchmarks by up to 12.1% with only 500 SFT steps. This is not a direct ScanQA/SQA3D selector and should not be transferred as the main method.

### Candidate refinement: QASER-TopoReserve
**Research hypothesis:** under a strict object budget, supporting-context slots selected to preserve local scene-graph topology around QA evidence will reduce context loss compared with semantic Top-K or pure geometric diversity.

**Changed stage:** QA-utility allocation / context reserve before PoseAlign-T and Abstract3D rendering.

**Object-selection rule:** hard-retain named seeds; generate unnamed answer/support candidates scene-wide; allocate primary slots by QA utility; use remaining reserve slots to preserve graph connectivity/topological coverage rather than nearest-neighbor distance alone.

**Supporting-context rule:** retain an unnamed object when it (a) completes a relation endpoint, (b) bridges seed and candidate in a sparse relation graph, (c) disambiguates same-class seeds, or (d) maximizes topology coverage under the remaining K budget. Topology is a prior/tie-breaker, not a hard gate.

**Expected benefit:** fewer disconnected/semantically narrow subscenes, especially for relational and multi-object questions, without rendering the full scene.

**Implementation difficulty:** low-medium. Build graph from fixed GT boxes/labels using cheap relation edges; no detector/segmenter and no VLM fine-tuning required for V0.

**Compute:** graph construction/scoring CPU-side; negligible VRAM beyond the existing <=7B VLM. Optional learned/Dirichlet variant is light-training only and should remain an ablation.

**Evaluation:** ScanQA/SQA3D QA metrics; Object Reduction Ratio; Question Entity Coverage; proxy Supporting-Context Retention; graph connectivity/coverage of selected subscene; QA-vs-object-budget at K={5,10,20}; context-loss cases.

**Ablations:** QAUtility vs QAUtility+random reserve vs +geometric-diversity reserve vs +TopoReserve; remove bridge retention; remove context reserve; +PoseAlign-T. Compare matched budgets against Full Scene, SemanticTopK, Seed+KNN, CAPruner, PruneGround-style, AgentGrounder-style.

**Risks:** proximity-derived graph topology can over-retain irrelevant objects; fixed box relations are imperfect; topology preservation itself is prior art and cannot be the novelty claim.

**Publication potential:** moderate as an internal refinement if it improves the QA-vs-object-budget Pareto frontier; weak as a standalone contribution.

**Scores (1-5):** Novelty 2.8 standalone / 3.5 integrated; Expected Accuracy Gain 3.7; Feasibility 4.8; Compute Cost 5.0 (higher=cheaper); Fit 5.0; Evidence Strength 4.7.

**Object-selection targets:** ORR 50–85%; QEC >=95%; Supporting-Context Retention proxy-only; context-loss risk medium.

## Secondary evidence: SpatialClaw
SpatialClaw is NeurIPS 2026 with official code (NVlabs/SpatialClaw). It is training-free and shows strong gains from iterative geometry-tool composition, but uses much larger backbones (reported 26B–397B range) and heavy perception services. Treat iterative tool/code reasoning as prior art and as evidence for a lightweight deterministic geometry verifier, not as thesis novelty or main implementation.

## Novelty boundary update
Do not claim topology preservation, graph-based spatial reasoning, iterative geometry-tool reasoning, or code-agent reasoning as the main novelty. The defensible claim remains QA-specific **pre-render** retention of named seeds plus unnamed answer/support/context objects under a strict object/render budget on ScanQA/SQA3D.

## Top candidate of the week
**QASER-PreRender / SAFER-QA**, with **TopoReserve** as the strongest new context-retention refinement to test.

Reason: it preserves the cleanest gap versus grounding-oriented pruning and post-render token compression, while TopoReserve offers a cheap way to address the main failure mode—losing unnamed context—without moving away from fixed GT objects, training-free inference, or <=7B 2D VLMs.
