## Prior work 1: Lens3D — Target-Conditioned Visual Foveation
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06611 (submitted 2026-10-05; arXiv preprint; no verified official repository or public LensBench data release).
- LensUnd: ground a target 3D box, rank depth-visible views, greedily select frames that add *new target-surface voxels*, mark the box, and query a 2D VLM. Its LensDistill student training is NOT training-free; the LensUnd inference procedure can be adapted without task training.
- With Qwen3-VL-2B on LensBench, summary F1 increases from 0.380 (baseline configuration) to 0.445 (LensUnd); the paper's different evidence/prompt configurations are not a controlled ScanQA comparison. Four-view bbox-voxel gives 0.445 vs ExCap3D visibility 0.436. LensBench: 2,068 ScanNet validation objects, silver reference descriptions. Paper also evaluates ScanQA/SQA3D as retained student abilities, not evidence of our proposed QA selector.
- Crucial novelty boundary: 3D box guided foveation, instance marking, and greedy marginal surface coverage are existing prior art. Do not claim them independently. Paper's full RGB-D video evidence violates the primary method's no-full-scene-render condition if copied unchanged.
- Compute: 2B external VLM is compatible with target backbone size; exact GPU/VRAM unreported. Full LensDistill needs student finetuning and multi-model teacher data; do not reproduce.

## Prior work 2: MuSe — Multi-Stage Graph Reasoning via VLMs
Source: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.476/ (ACL main, July 2026; official code not verified).
- Incrementally samples and visualizes query-relevant subgraphs; trains Qwen2.5-VL-7B with SFT + GRPO. Uses 8 x A800 80GB; not feasible for main training-free path.
- Graph benchmark GVLQA/LGVLQA, not ScanQA/SQA3D. Reported average inference latency 19.88 s vs direct Qwen2.5-VL-7B 3.82 s in its setup. Do not transfer numeric gains to 3D QA.
- Novelty boundary: task-adaptive subgraph visualization and iterative retrieval are established; consider only as conceptual control for adaptive expansion.

## Candidate: RoleSurface-CoVisibility (refines Selected-Witness CoVisibility)
Research hypothesis: after question-conditioned pre-render selection of named seeds and unnamed answer/support objects, choosing views that jointly reveal the *actual visible surfaces* of all indispensable witness roles will reduce evidence loss versus center/box-based view ranking at fixed K and M.
Stage: PoseAlign-T -> view selection -> 3D Abstract Renderer; not the core selector.
Object-selection rule: inherit selected S from SAFER-QA; no unselected object can enter a render. Seeds identified from explicit question entities; unnamed answer endpoints, relation witnesses, competing candidates and reference-frame supports found scene-wide with soft semantic/geometry scores; no exact-noun/KNN hard gate.
Supporting-context rule: preserve seed + supporting witnesses jointly in the view objective. For each candidate pose v, approximate visible instance-mesh surface cells V(v,o) for selected objects only; compute a weighted union coverage per role, penalize a view that hides the only answer witness. Preserve an overview view for spatial context. No full-scene rendering.
Proposed objective: choose at most M poses maximizing sum over selected role objects o of w(q,o)*|union_{v in poses} V(v,o)|/max(1,|surfaceCells(o)|) minus redundancy penalty. This is our proposed extension, NOT Lens3D's original objective. Check co-visibility for relational questions.
Expected benefit: fewer selected-but-invisible witnesses, especially small or occluded objects; accuracy gain unknown.
Implementation: medium. Start with low-resolution depth-only z-buffer for selected instance meshes, optionally coarse mesh sampling; no additional model training. CPU or GPU rasterizer; added VRAM estimated <1GB for low-res visibility buffers but NOT measured. VLM peak VRAM depends on 2B/4B quantization, image resolution and M; measure.
Data/metrics: ScanQA validation, SQA3D validation; QA CIDEr/EM; ORR=1-|S|/N; Question Entity Coverage; Supporting-Context Retention (proxy until independently labeled); answer-witness recall; role co-visibility; surface coverage; context loss; VLM calls, latency, peak VRAM.
Ablations: fixed PoseAlign-T vs center/box co-visibility vs mesh-surface co-visibility, K={5,10,20}, M={1,2}; identical selected objects, prompt, VLM and render resolution; paired object-support removal control. Separate relational vs attribute QA. Keep budget equal.
Risks: selected-only depth omits occlusion by unselected physical objects, so estimated visibility can be optimistic; high context loss at K=5; mesh artifacts; surface coverage may favor visually large distractors. Publication potential LOW standalone; MEDIUM only as SAFER-QA refinement with downstream evidence.
Scores (/5, higher compute score=cheaper): Novelty 2.2, Expected Accuracy Gain 3.3, Feasibility 4.4, Compute Cost 4.3, Fit 4.8, Evidence Strength 4.1. Scores are research prioritization, not benchmark results.

## Core object-selection track
Main hypothesis remains QA-specific pre-render retention of explicit named seeds plus unnamed answer/support/context objects under strict object budget. Preferred V0: frozen semantic seed retrieval -> typed QA slots -> scene-wide soft relation candidates -> role-aware witness bundle allocation -> PoseAlign-T -> selected-instance-mesh rendering -> <=7B 2D VLM. Evaluate semantic Top-K, FARM-like soft retrieval, CAPruner/GraFT-inspired controls at matched K, M, model, prompt and calls.
Metrics: ORR target 50–85%, QEC target >=95% are unverified goals; SCR is proxy unless independent support GT exists. Track risk of context loss, especially count/existence questions where complete evidence may exceed K. Compare text-only, label+box-only, selected render, and support-removal to test visual dependence.
