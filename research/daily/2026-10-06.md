# Daily Proposal Research — 2026-10-06

## New high-value evidence: SceneScaffold (NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight)

SceneScaffold: Active Scene-State Construction for Unified 3D Scene Understanding (arXiv:2609.33518; NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight; official code: https://github.com/lixiangqi707/SceneScaffold).

Verified implementation facts:
- Role-aware fixed-budget scene states: entity (8), scene-frame (8), relation (8), global scene (1), plus residual detail tokens; K=100.
- Evaluates ScanQA and SQA3D directly.
- Uses LLaVA-1.5-7B and an Omni Superpoint Transformer.
- Requires LoRA instruction tuning; point-cloud encoder and LLM body are frozen, but scene-state/projection/LoRA modules are trained.
- Repository reports ScanQA CIDEr 95.8 and SQA3D EM 55.5 / EM-R 58.3, vs 3D-LLaVA 92.6 and 54.5 / 56.6 under the reported comparison.
- Training setup is materially heavier than our preferred training-free pipeline (repo example uses 8 GPUs; environment tested on A800s).

## Novelty implication

SceneScaffold makes generic role-aware context preservation (entity/frame/relation/global) non-novel. However, it compresses/organizes already encoded superpoint evidence under a token budget. Our remaining gap is earlier in the pipeline: decide which fixed annotated objects are worth rendering at all, conditioned on downstream QA.

## Candidate refinement: QASER-RoleBudget

Research hypothesis: allocating a strict pre-render object budget by QA role (seed / answer-support / spatial-frame / global-context) can preserve relation-intensive QA better than flat semantic Top-K while avoiding full-scene rendering.

Stage changed:
question-conditioned object selection before PoseAlign-T / Abstract3D.

Object-selection rule:
1. Hard-retain named seeds.
2. Generate scene-wide unnamed answer/support candidates from relation intent and cheap GT-box geometry.
3. Allocate K by role, e.g. K=10: 2 seed/disambiguation + 5 answer/relation support + 2 spatial-frame anchors + 1 global/context reserve; reallocate unused slots dynamically.
4. Geometry is verifier/reranker, not a universal KNN hard gate.
5. PoseAlign-T resolves viewpoint-dependent directional relations before final pruning/rendering.

Supporting-context rule:
unnamed objects are retained if they can complete the queried relation, disambiguate a seed, serve as a spatial-frame anchor, or provide non-redundant global context. Exact noun match is never sufficient as the selector.

Expected benefit:
better QA-vs-object-budget frontier, especially relation-intensive/ambiguous questions; role allocation may reduce catastrophic context loss at small K.

Implementation difficulty:
low-medium; training-free V0 can operate on GT labels/boxes and a small <=7B parser/VLM.

Compute:
selector largely CPU/cheap embedding inference; negligible extra VRAM beyond <=7B VLM. No SceneScaffold-style 3D training required.

Evaluation:
ScanQA CIDEr/BLEU/METEOR/ROUGE/EM; SQA3D EM/EM-R; Object Reduction Ratio; Question Entity Coverage; proxy Supporting-Context Retention; Context Benefit Rate; QA-vs-object-budget; rendered views and VLM calls.

Ablation:
Flat SemanticTopK vs Seed+KNN vs relation-aware support vs QASER-RoleBudget; remove frame reserve; remove global reserve; equal role allocation vs dynamic allocation; K={5,10,20}; +PoseAlign-T. Compare conceptually/where feasible against SceneScaffold as post-encoding fixed-budget organization, but do not claim apples-to-apples efficiency without matching input/compute.

Risk:
role labels may be noisy; fixed quotas can waste slots; frame/global anchors may not help attribute-only questions. Mitigate with question-type-conditioned dynamic reallocation.

Publication potential:
moderate. Role-aware budgeting alone is not novel after SceneScaffold; defensible contribution remains QA-specific pre-render object selection and supporting-object retention under strict render budget.

Scores (1-5):
Novelty 3.3 integrated; Expected Accuracy Gain 3.8; Feasibility 4.8; Compute Cost 4.9 (higher=cheaper); Fit 5.0; Evidence Strength 5.0.
Targets to test, not claims: ORR 50-85%; QEC >=95%; Supporting-Context Retention proxy-only; context-loss risk medium.

## Secondary evidence

A peer-reviewed Complex & Intelligent Systems article, "3D visual grounding based on active perception" (published 2026-09-10), maintains candidate sets and structured evidence, acquires informative views, verifies only local candidates, and expands/backtracks when evidence is ambiguous/missing/conflicting. It supports uncertainty-gated local expansion, but also tightens the novelty boundary: closed-loop candidate verification / active view acquisition is prior art and should remain an optional refinement, not the thesis claim.

## Top candidate this week

QASER-PreRender / SAFER-QA remains #1.

Best current form:
seed retrieval -> scene-wide unnamed QA-support generation -> QA-role budget allocation (support + frame/context reserve) -> geometry verification -> PoseAlign-T directional refinement -> selective Abstract3D render -> <=7B 2D VLM.

Why #1:
SceneScaffold provides unusually strong direct ScanQA/SQA3D evidence that preserving entity, relation, spatial-frame, and global roles matters, but it does so after 3D feature encoding and with LoRA training. The cleaner remaining gap is pre-render QA-specific selection: avoid paying rendering/encoding cost for irrelevant objects while retaining unnamed answer/support/context objects needed downstream.
