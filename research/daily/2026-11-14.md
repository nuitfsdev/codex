## Candidate: Question-Conditioned Distractor Stress Test (QCDST) — evaluation only
**Hypothesis:** Even when seed and answer/support witness coverage is unchanged, semantically plausible but irrelevant rendered objects can lower 2D VLM QA accuracy; selectively removing distractors may improve the QA/object-budget frontier.
**Stage:** question-conditioned selection evaluation, renderer, multi-view aggregation.
**Selection rule:** Use fixed object ID/box table; identify named seed via semantic matching, search unnamed answer candidates scene-wide using soft geometry/semantic scores (not exact nouns or KNN hard filter); keep |S|<=K and render only S.
**Supporting-context rule:** preserve independently annotated relation endpoints, alternative witness bundles, reference anchors and same-class disambiguators. Construct paired S_good vs S_distractor of equal K, replacing only confirmed nonessential objects; do not use answer/support GT in deployed selection.
**Expected benefit:** diagnostic evidence about distraction; potential guidance for QA-specific budget allocation, no proven gain.
**Implementation:** medium; CPU selection O(N^2), no selector GPU, additional inference with <=7B 4-bit VLM; peak VRAM unknown, T4 16GB target not verified.
**Data/metrics:** 50–100 independently annotated ScanQA validation questions then SQA3D; EM/CIDEr, ORR=1-|S|/N, QEC, Answer-Witness Recall, SCR (GT only if independently labeled), Complete-Witness Coverage, context-loss rate, latency, peak VRAM, token/call count.
**Ablations:** same K=10 M=1 and camera, prompt, renderer, backbone; semantic Top-K vs OpenSlotSoft vs Role-Aware; paired replace-distractor vs replace-support vs random, K=5/20 and M=2 follow-ups. Distinguish occlusion and cropping confounds.
**Risks:** falsely labeled distractors; missing alternative witnesses; counting/negation require global completeness; rendered object swaps alter image composition. High context-loss risk at K=5.
**Publication:** low standalone, moderate as rigorous evidence for SAFER-QA only if matched-budget QA gain demonstrated.
**Scores /5 (Compute higher=cheaper):** Novelty 1.2, Expected Accuracy Gain 2.3, Feasibility 4.5, Compute 4.3, Fit 4.9, Evidence 4.2. Subjective priority scores, not benchmarks.
