## Direct-prior novelty audit (2 Nov; papers older, newly checked, NOT new 2 Nov arXiv)
- **ViewMind3D** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28442 submitted 30 Jul, arXiv preprint, no official code/peer-reviewed venue verified. Training-free modular question-based multi-view selection + guided detection + BEV camera indicators + role-based reasoning; uses GPT-4.1/o3, NOT local <=7B; ScanQA CIDEr 73.41 (o3), SQA3D 50.75% (o3); ~90K tokens/~120s/query reported. **Direct prior for modular training-free 2D-VLM 3D-QA, question-based view selection, structured reasoning.** It starts from RGB full-scene views, NOT strict pre-render physical-object selection from fixed boxes.
- **Scene-Q** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20235 ; official https://teamrobi.github.io/projects/scene-q/ and code https://github.com/teamROBI/Scene-Q identify **IROS 2026** (authors' site, proceedings not independently checked). Temperature-calibrated CLIP/SigLIP2 confidence, selective Qwen2.5-VL-7B escalation, ScanNet200/real-world instance retrieval; **NOT ScanQA**. Author-reported oracle-mask SigLIP2 mAP no/selective/always = 48.4/51.9/50.8; not QA. Full segmentation pipeline OUT OF SCOPE; confidence-gated VLM is PRIOR ART. Repo runnable code/configs, original VRAM unknown.
- **Seeing Once is Enough?** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04079 submitted 5 Jul; author https://rclai.cc/ lists ICLR 2026 **Workshop** (not main), code not verified. Training-free RGB-D+pose geometry-aware **visual-token** overlap pruning, up to 50% token reduction on ScanQA/SQA3D/OpenEQA, Qwen2.5-VL-7B/Qwen3-VL-8B. NOT physical-object pre-render selection. Useful later efficiency ablation only.
- Freshness: arXiv official cs.CV /new and cs.AI /new still show **Friday 30 Oct** at 10:04 on Mon 2 Nov; no verified 2 Nov paper batch. Watchlist only: author page https://rclai.cc/ lists *The Key is the Question* under review but accessible arXiv/code not confirmed.

## New secondary candidate detail
**QRCWG**: role-parse question to separate explicit seeds vs scene-wide unnamed answer/support/bridge/context; frozen semantic retrieval for seeds; soft box geometry and role-specific relation constraints to rank unnamed witnesses; deterministic gate changes support reserve 0/10/20/30% when open answer slot, same-class ambiguity or multi-hop. No exact noun-only or KNN hard gate. |S|<=K pre-render. No training V0, CPU O(N^2) worst-case, ~0 extra selector GPU VRAM, T4 16GB VLM feasibility unmeasured. Metrics ScanQA/SQA3D QA EM/CIDEr, ORR/QEC/AWR/independent-GT SCR or proxy/Context-Loss Rate/latency/VRAM. Matched K=10 M=1 Top-K/OpenSlotSoft/RWC/gate-off/gate-on; K=5/20 later. Context-loss HIGH K=5, especially counting/negative. Publication LOW alone / MEDIUM only integrated if matched-budget QA gain.
**IAVOB**: preserve S; choose M views maximizing unique selected witness ID/relation-edge visibility and penalizing duplicate selected-object observations; no full-scene render; training-free CPU geometry, optional low-res selected-mesh visibility, M=1/2 ablation vs random/coverage/pose-only. Metrics QA, selected witness visibility, view overlap, tokens, latency, inherited ORR/QEC/AWR/SCR. Occlusion estimation/context-loss risk; LOW standalone publication.

## Object-selection evaluation invariants
ORR = 1 - |S|/N; QEC explicit named seed GT coverage; AWR unnamed answer GT recall; SCR independent supporting-context GT recall (otherwise **PROXY**, never claim certified GT); complete witness coverage, context loss, QA-vs-object-budget Pareto, scene-clustered CIs, peak VRAM, VLM calls and latency. ORR 50–85% and QEC >=95% **UNVERIFIED TARGETS**. K=5 HIGH context-loss; counting/existence/negative questions need completeness check and abstention, not assume selected S equals scene. Never use ScanQA relevance labels or test GT in selector. Visual-only/label-only/box-only ablation to detect metadata shortcuts.
