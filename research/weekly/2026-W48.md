## New prior-art correction
AgentGrounder (May 2026, ICRA 2026 MM-Spatial & SRRA workshops; https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25901; https://github.com/be2rlab/AgentGrounder) already implements offline OLT + selective candidate retrieval + geometry + optional on-demand rendering. Strong overlap with generic pre-render object selection. It is grounding on ScanRefer/Nr3D, not open-ended ScanQA/SQA3D. This narrows defensible novelty to QA-specific unnamed answer/support witnesses and evidence sufficiency under strict object budget. Do not claim selective rendering or tool-driven geometric candidate retrieval as new.

## 2026-11-24 — view retrieval audit (secondary)
MemTree3D (arXiv 2608.18009, 18 Aug 2026; ECCV 2026 per https://www.amazon.science/publications/memory-tree-guided-key-frame-querying-for-efficient-3d-question-answering; code https://github.com/hsiangwei0903/MemTree3D) uses reusable hierarchical 3D memory and pose-aware keyframe retrieval on OpenEQA. ## 2026-11-25 — ViewAgent3D (fresh preprint)
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28673 (v1 21 Nov 2026), full text https://arxiv.org/html/2611.28673v1 §§2.1–2.4. Training-free RGB-only pipeline: structured concept decomposition, SigLIP2 retrieval, MMR diversity, VLM answerability reranking, optional GroundingDINO, bounded self-verification. Peer-reviewed venue, official code and peak VRAM unverified. Generic answerability reranking and verification are prior art, not novelty. Candidate refinement: role-complementary object retrieval, using marginal coverage of named seed and open answer/support roles rather than global semantic similarity or generic diversity. Keep fixed annotations, strict K, no full-scene render. Score marginal role coverage using soft semantic/geometric compatibility; preserve competing anchors; compare semantic Top-K, MMR, SAFER-QA and role coverage at K=10 M=1 on ScanQA/SQA3D. CPU O(KNR) with precomputed role scores, no additional GPU; accuracy unmeasured, risk of wrong role parsing and incomplete counting. ORR=1-K/N, QEC/SCR require independent labels, report EM/CIDEr, witness recall, latency/VRAM. Scores N1.5/G3.2/F4.5/C4.8/Fit5/E3.5 (subjective); merge into Role-Aware Witness Coverage rather than new track. Publication potential low alone. SAFER-QA remains top pending matched-budget evaluation. No GPU or secrets.

Compare single-frame vs multi-frame reserve at K=10, M=1, ScanQA/SQA3D directional subset; measure EM/CIDEr, ORR, QEC, SCR, witness recall and context loss; no measured gain. Scores N1.6/G3.1/F4.5/C4.8/Fit5/E3.5, subjective. Risk: wrong frames consume budget; do not confuse render camera with semantic reference frame. Low standalone publication potential.
